# Online Adaptive Testing


***Online Adaptive Testing*** is a web-based platform designed to enhance the learning experience by offering customized assessments tailored to individual student needs. The platform dynamically adjusts the difficulty level of questions based on the student's performance, ensuring an optimal challenge level that promotes growth and understanding. It’s ideal for educators seeking to provide targeted feedback and students looking to assess their knowledge in a more personalized and effective way.
The platform provides pesonalized feedback to the students on their performance.

**Introduction**

***Online Adaptive Testing*** is an  educational platform designed to revolutionize the way students engage with assessments. Traditional testing methods often fail to address the varying needs and abilities of individual learners, leading to a one-size-fits-all approach that can hinder both high-performing and struggling students.

This project leverages adaptive testing techniques to create a more dynamic and responsive assessment environment. By analyzing a student’s responses in real-time, the platform adjusts the difficulty of subsequent questions to match the student’s current understanding. This not only helps maintain the right level of challenge but also ensures that students are neither overwhelmed nor under-challenged.

In addition to adaptive question selection, students receive personalized feedback on their performance. This feedback includes a detailed analysis of their overall performance in the test, highlighting areas of strength as well as areas for improvement.

It is a prototype we have designed to achieve the functionality of adaptive testing.
